The Problem

Shift work is the largest occupational health crisis nobody talks about

Night shifts don't just disrupt your schedule — they disrupt your biology. Your body evolved for a solar day. Working against that clock means chronic sleep debt, metabolic disruption, and cognitive impairment that compounding over years. Yet the standard advice is "just sleep when you can."

That's not a solution. That's abandonment.

Features

Everything your circadian rhythm needs

Every feature traces to published, peer-reviewed research. Not wellness blogs. Not vibes.

😴

Adaptive Sleep Windows

Personalized sleep and wake times based on the Two-Process Model (Borbely 1982). Adjusts automatically when your schedule changes — including the tricky transition days between day and night shifts.

Based on AASM Guidelines
☕

Smart Caffeine Cutoffs

Caffeine has a 5-hour half-life. ShiftWell calculates your personal cutoff time based on your sensitivity, shift timing, and target sleep window — so you get the boost without the insomnia.

Based on Drake et al. 2013
🍽️

Circadian Meal Timing

When you eat matters as much as what you eat. ShiftWell schedules your eating windows to align with your circadian rhythm, reducing metabolic disruption from off-hours eating.

Based on Manoogian et al. 2022
💡

Light Exposure Protocols

Strategic bright light at the right times can shift your circadian clock by 1–3 hours per day. ShiftWell tells you exactly when to seek and avoid light based on your transition schedule.
