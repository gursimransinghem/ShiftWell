Health & Medical Disclaimers

Last reviewed 2026-04-20

ShiftWell is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Sleep schedule recommendations are for informational purposes only and are not a substitute for professional medical guidance. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making changes to your sleep habits, especially if you have a diagnosed sleep disorder or other medical condition.

General wellness product

ShiftWell is a general wellness application intended to help shift workers manage their sleep schedules. It qualifies under the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's General Wellness Policy as a low-risk general wellness product. ShiftWell is not a medical device as defined under Section 201(h) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. It has not been evaluated, cleared, or approved by the FDA or any other regulatory body.

Not medical advice

The sleep schedule recommendations, light exposure guidance, nap timing, caffeine cutoffs, and other information provided by ShiftWell are for general informational and wellness purposes only. Nothing in this app constitutes medical advice, professional diagnosis, or a professional treatment recommendation.

ShiftWell's recommendations are informed by published scientific research — including the Two-Process Model of sleep regulation (Borbely, 1982), shift-work circadian adaptation protocols (Eastman & Burgess, 2009), and shift-work epidemiology (Drake et al., 2013) — but these recommendations have not been independently validated in clinical trials of ShiftWell specifically.

Not a substitute for professional care

ShiftWell is not a substitute for the advice, diagnosis, or treatment provided by a licensed physician, sleep specialist, or other qualified healthcare professional. Users should not disregard professional medical advice, delay seeking medical care, or discontinue any prescribed treatment based on information provided by ShiftWell.

No diagnosis or treatment of Shift Work Sleep Disorder

ShiftWell does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent Shift Work Sleep Disorder (SWSD) or any other medical condition. If you experience persistent insomnia, excessive sleepiness, difficulty concentrating, or other symptoms consistent with SWSD, please seek evaluation from a qualified healthcare provider. Prevalence data (Drake et al., 2013) suggests a significant proportion of shift workers may meet SWSD criteria — a recognized clinical diagnosis that requires professional assessment.

Individual results vary

Individual responses to sleep schedule adjustments vary significantly based on chronotype, age, health status, shift schedule, caffeine metabolism, medication use, and other factors. ShiftWell makes no guarantee that its recommendations will improve your sleep quality, sleep duration, alertness, or overall health. Results described in the scientific literature represent population-level findings and may not reflect any individual user's experience.

Shift-worker safety — drowsy driving and workplace hazards

Never drive or operate heavy machinery if you feel drowsy, regardless of whether you have followed ShiftWell's recommendations. Shift work is associated with increased risk of fatigue-related accidents including drowsy driving, workplace injuries, and medication errors.

ShiftWell's recommendations are intended to support better sleep habits but cannot eliminate fatigue risk. Users must:

  • Never drive or operate heavy machinery while drowsy, regardless of adherence to the app's plan
  • Follow all employer safety protocols and workplace fatigue management policies
  • Be aware that transitioning between shift schedules can temporarily increase fatigue, even when following an optimized plan

If you experience excessive sleepiness while driving or working, stop immediately and seek a safe environment. ShiftWell assumes no responsibility for accidents, injuries, or safety incidents.

You should evaluate all recommendations in the context of your personal health status and, where appropriate, in consultation with your healthcare provider.
