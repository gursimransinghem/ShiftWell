Frequently Asked Questions

How does ShiftWell protect employee privacy?

+
Individual health data (sleep logs, fatigue scores, recommendations) remains private and under employee control. Your organization receives only anonymized, aggregate insights: fatigue risk trends by unit or shift type, participation rates, and compliance documentation. We never share identifying information, location data, or individual health records with employers. This is fundamental to our architecture and privacy promise.

Does ShiftWell integrate with our scheduling system?

+
Yes. We have API connectors for QGenda, Kronos, and other major scheduling platforms. Schedules sync automatically, keeping recommendations fresh. If you use a system we don't yet support, we offer manual CSV import or can work with your IT team on a custom connector. Our team provides full setup and ongoing support during deployment.
