Frequently Asked Questions

Does ShiftWell support 24/48 and 48/96 schedules?

Yes. ShiftWell supports all common first responder rotations: 24/48 (24 hours on, 48 hours off), 48/96 (48 hours on, 96 hours off), Kelly schedules, ABCD rotations, and any custom pattern you define. You input your exact rotation, and ShiftWell generates a personalized sleep plan for each phase of your cycle.

How do I deal with interrupted sleep at the station?

ShiftWell uses strategic pre-positioning: it tells you when to sleep before your shift starts (pre-sleep), optimal nap windows during the shift, and post-call recovery timing. The app factors in that station sleep is fragmented and provides nap-banking strategies to accumulate sleep debt across your tour. Studies show pre-positioned sleep reduces fatigue risk by 20–30%.

I can't sleep after a high-adrenaline call. Can ShiftWell help?

Absolutely. ShiftWell includes a Post-Adrenaline Wind-Down Protocol with guidance on light exposure, temperature management, breathing techniques, and timing of caffeine/nutrition to help your nervous system recover after high-stress calls. The science is based on Two-Process Model research and NIOSH fatigue risk management. Many users see 30–45 minutes faster sleep onset post-call.

Is ShiftWell just another sleep tracker?

No. ShiftWell is predictive and prescriptive. Most sleep apps track what happened last night. ShiftWell looks at your next 14 days of shift rotations and generates a proactive sleep plan—when to nap, when to light-expose, when to eat, and how to sync it all to your calendar. It's built by circadian science and designed specifically for shift work, not generic sleep tracking.
