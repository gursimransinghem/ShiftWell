Questions? We've Got Answers

How does ShiftWell handle rotating schedules?
ShiftWell uses a deterministic algorithm based on the Two-Process Model—the same science that powers AASM sleep medicine guidelines. Instead of one generic plan, we calculate your individual sleep pressure accumulation and circadian phase for each day in your schedule. As your shifts rotate, your plan adapts. Whether you work 8-hour, 10-hour, 12-hour, or 24-hour shifts (or a mix), ShiftWell learns your pattern and predicts optimal sleep windows weeks in advance.
Can I use ShiftWell if I work 12-hour shifts?
Absolutely. ShiftWell is specifically designed for 12-hour shifts. We optimize for the unique challenges of 12-hour rotations: maintaining circadian alignment through quick turnarounds, preserving sleep quality during day sleep after night shifts, and protecting your days off so you can be present with family. Nurses working 12-hour days and nights are our primary focus.
Will ShiftWell help me sleep after a night shift?
Yes. Post-shift sleep is one of ShiftWell's core specializations. After a night shift, your circadian system is still signaling "stay awake." We provide: (1) precise timing for dark sunglasses and light avoidance on your way home, (2) optimal sleep window recommendations when your body is most likely to consolidate sleep, (3) temperature and caffeine guidance to support daytime sleep quality, and (4) light exposure timing for your next shift to reset your rhythm faster.
Is the science behind ShiftWell evidence-based?
Completely. ShiftWell's algorithm is built on the Two-Process Model of sleep regulation (Borbely, 1982), validated by AASM sleep medicine guidelines, and incorporates circadian shifting protocols from Eastman & Burgess (2009). We cite Drake et al. (2004) on shift work sleep disorder prevalence, Czeisler et al. (1990) on light exposure timing, and Boivin & Boudreau (2014) on evidence-based interventions. Every recommendation traces to peer-reviewed research. No LLM hallucinations. Just math and science.
