# Lane 1 — Fulcrum / Scale Minimalism — Rationale

## The idea

Three nodes on a horizontal beam, balanced on a downward gold triangle. That is the entire mark. The three nodes ARE Work, Life, Sleep — spatially arranged in equilibrium on a precision instrument, not a symbol of one.

## Why it hooks attention

- **The triangle points down.** Every scale-of-justice logo points up — the fulcrum supports the beam from below. Here the pivot descends beneath the beam, which reads as tension and precision rather than courtroom gravity. It's the same form, rotated, and suddenly ownable.
- **One accent, one place.** The gold triangle is the only color element. The eye finds it immediately. It is the pivot — literally and hierarchically.
- **The center node wears a ring.** Two nodes are plain white dots (Work, Sleep — routine states). The center node (Life) has a gold circumference ring — the thing being protected. This distinction is not decoration; it encodes meaning.

## Why people would wear it on a T-shirt

The mark is structurally confident and abstract enough that it reads as a precision-instrument insignia rather than an app icon. It belongs on the sleeve of a flight jacket as naturally as on a phone screen. A nurse would wear it because it says "instrument" not "app." A pilot would wear it because it says "calibrated" not "branded."

## Scalability notes

At 1024px: all proportions hold; the gold ring on the center node reads clearly with an 8.5px stroke. The pivot triangle is 80px tall — dominant but not overwhelming.

At 32px (favicon): the gold ring is dropped; three dots on a beam above a small gold triangle remain fully legible at 2× screen density. The beam-over-triangle silhouette is distinct at thumbnail scale — it does not collapse into a blob.

## Anti-cliché checklist

- No z's, pillows, clocks, moons, crescents — not present
- No soft pastel gradients, meditation spirals — not present (zero gradients used)
- No stethoscope, caduceus, cross, heart-rate line — not present
- No scales-of-justice (the triangle inverts the convention, making it unrecognizable as legal iconography)
- No 3D effects, skeuomorphism, drop shadows — not present
- Survives 1-color monochrome at 32px — yes (white beam + dots on dark; gold can be same value)
- Wearable on a black T-shirt — yes; the mark reads as a technical insignia

## What I deliberately didn't do (and why)

No text labels inside the mark. The three nodes do not say "W / L / S" — labeling them would condescend to the viewer and collapse the abstraction into a diagram. The meaning is in the geometry, not the annotation.

No arc or wave form beneath the beam. A circadian wave would be thematically accurate but visually redundant — the app name communicates the domain; the mark communicates the value (balance). Adding a wave is explaining the joke.

No gradient on the triangle. The brief permits palette colors; a purple-to-gold gradient on the pivot would look like every mHealth app icon on the store. Flat gold is rarer and more premium at this scale.
