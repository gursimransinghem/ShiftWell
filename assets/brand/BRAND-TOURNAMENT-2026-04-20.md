## Scoring rubric (my read)

Scale 1–5. Numbers are gut reactions from reading the rationales + inspecting the SVGs; founder's pick overrides.

|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|

| 4 — Möbius | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3.75 |
| 5 — SW Wordmark | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3.50 |

**Rubric definitions:**
- **Memorable:** Would a stranger remember this after one glance?
- **Scalable:** Does the 32×32 favicon still read as the brand?
- **On-brand:** Does it honor the 7 principles (no clichés, precision-forward, no wellness-speak)?
- **Wearable:** Would a shift worker be proud to wear this on a T-shirt or phone case?

**My top 2:**
1. **Lane 1 (Fulcrum)** — Highest overall. The downward-pointing triangle is a real "ownable" move — it inverts the scales-of-justice cliché and becomes unrecognizable as legal iconography. Reads as precision instrument. Favicon test: passes cleanly.
2. **Lane 2 (Bioluminescent)** — Most visually arresting. Weakest on favicon (gradients collapse), but strongest at hero sizes. The "what IS that?" hook is the most powerful conversation-starter of the 5.

**Lane 3 (Astronomical)** is the dark horse — its viral mechanic (concealed monogram reveal) is the strongest social-sharing move, but it loses at 32px where stars disappear and only the crescent survives (which could be mistaken for another sleep app).

**Lane 4 (Möbius)** is intellectually elegant but visually easy to confuse with a figure-8 infinity loop at a glance. The payoff requires the viewer to study it — which is a high bar for a logo.

**Lane 5 (SW Wordmark)** is the safest choice and the weakest creative swing. Wordmarks die at 32px in ways the designer acknowledges (the purple joint helps but isn't enough). Recommended only if the founder specifically wants a pure typographic mark.

## Lane 1 — Fulcrum / Scale Minimalism

**Files:** `explorations/lane-1-fulcrum/`

**ASCII sketch:**
```
        ●────────●────────●        ← 3 nodes: Work · Life · Sleep
                 ◯                  ← center node wears gold ring
                 ▼                  ← downward gold fulcrum triangle
```

**Key rationale quote:**
> "Every scale-of-justice logo points up — the fulcrum supports the beam from below. Here the pivot descends beneath the beam, which reads as tension and precision rather than courtroom gravity. It's the same form, rotated, and suddenly ownable."

**What's strong:**
- One accent, one place (gold triangle only)
- Center node wears gold ring — encodes meaning (Life is the protected thing)
- Beam-over-triangle silhouette survives favicon test

**What to watch:**
- Could read as "abstract dot pattern" without context — needs the wordmark lockup to cement it
- Three-dot compositions are increasingly common (Airtable, Loom) — differentiation depends on the inverted triangle

## Lane 2 — Bioluminescent Circadian Curve

**Files:** `explorations/lane-2-bioluminescent/`

**ASCII sketch:**
```
           ·◉··                     ← dawn node (gold, small halo)
        ∿∿∿∿∿∿∿∿                     ← 24h cosine curve
      ∿    ◉    ∿                   ← night node (purple, large halo)
     ∿             ∿
    ∿               ∿
   ──────── 24h ────────             ← faint grid baseline
```

**Key rationale quote:**
> "Three mathematically distinct stroke widths (28px outer halo / 14px mid / 5px inner / 2px core) simulate actual bioluminescence physics. This is perceivable even without conscious attention: the mark has depth, not blur."

**What's strong:**
- Gradient carries data meaning (purple = melatonin peak, gold = dawn entrainment)
- Four concentric luminosity layers = structural glow, not filter blur
- Hero size (1024px, chest size on T-shirt) is unmatched

**What to watch:**
- Favicon (32px) collapses to a single purple stroke — survives, but loses the "wow"
- On white background the gradient transforms (designer solved this but it's a real constraint)
- Risk of drifting toward "wellness app" at a quick glance if rendered poorly downstream

## Lane 3 — Astronomical / Hidden "S" Monogram

**Files:** `explorations/lane-3-astronomical/`

**ASCII sketch:**
```
         *   *
      *           ← stars A–C form upper S-bowl
       *
      ((           ← crescent moon, shadow terminator = S-inflection
        ))
          *
             *    ← stars E–G form lower S-bowl
          *
```

**Key rationale quote:**
> "The S is therefore built from: stellar curve → lunar geometry → stellar curve. A faint dashed spine traces the full S-path but reads as a distant orbital arc rather than a letterform guide — visible to someone who already knows the secret, invisible to someone who doesn't."

**What's strong:**
- Viral mechanic: the reveal moment IS the pitch ("oh! it's an S!")
- NASA mission-patch aesthetic — engineering-serious, not mystical
- Ink-on-paper light variant is a genuinely clever solve

**What to watch:**
- Favicon drops most of the stars; mark becomes "just a crescent" at 32px
- Crescent + stars is the closest to sleep-iconography cliché of any lane (the designer defended this, but it's a real risk)
- Depends on the viewer caring enough to look twice

## Lane 4 — Möbius Sleep-Wake Cycle

**Files:** `explorations/lane-4-mobius/`

**ASCII sketch:**
```
        ╱─────╲
       ╱       ╲                    ← purple lobe (sleep, u=π→2π)
      │   ╳    │                    ← crossing point with over/under
       ╲       ╱
        ╲─────╱
        ╱─────╲                    ← gold lobe (wake, u=0→π)
       ╱       ╲
```

**Key rationale quote:**
> "This is not an infinity symbol. A Möbius strip is a non-orientable surface: cut it down the centerline and it does not become two separate loops; it becomes one double-length loop. That single-surface property is the load-bearing metaphor."

**What's strong:**
- Mathematically-faithful parametric projection (not cheated with overlapping ovals)
- Purple/gold split carries circadian phase data meaning
- Favicon uses knockout gap at crossing to preserve twist legibility

**What to watch:**
- First-glance read is "figure-8 infinity" — the Möbius distinction requires attention
- Risk of looking busy at chest-on-T-shirt scale
- Intellectual payoff is high but requires a viewer who notices the twist

## Lane 5 — SW Ligature / Geometric Wordmark

**Files:** `explorations/lane-5-wordmark/`
**Bonus file:** `construction-grid.svg` — shows the geometric derivation proof

**ASCII sketch:**
```
   ╭─╮│╲   ╱│╲   ╱
   │ ││ ╲ ╱ │ ╲ ╱     ← S (left) + W (right)
   ╰─╯│  v  │  v
      │                ← shared vertical purple joint (x=64)
      │
   ╭─╮                 ← S continues
   ╰─╯
```

**Key rationale quote:**
> "The S's lower-right terminal extends upward to the W's top-left entry point — a single vertical stroke at x=64, shared by both letters. That stroke is rendered in purple — the one moment of color in the entire mark. Its conceptual meaning: the boundary between states. The shift between awake and asleep. It is not explained anywhere. It does not need to be."

**What's strong:**
- Construction-grid SVG proves the geometry is systematic, not arbitrary
- Favicon tested down to 32px (wordmarks usually die at 48px)
- Purple joint doubles as conceptual symbolism (transition boundary)

**What to watch:**
- Wordmarks are the safest lane, which makes them the least memorable
- "SW" as initials is a common startup trope (even with this concept's discipline)
- Deliberately avoided illustration — strength for brand consistency, weakness for viral attention
