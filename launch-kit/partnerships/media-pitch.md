[JOURNALIST_FIRST_NAME],

I'm an ER doctor in Tampa who works rotating day and night shifts. After years of watching colleagues — nurses, residents, paramedics — wreck their health trying to sleep between 12-hour shifts with zero guidance beyond "take melatonin and hope for the best," I built something better.

**ShiftWell** is an iOS app that imports a shift worker's actual schedule from their calendar and generates a science-backed circadian optimization plan: when to sleep, when to nap, when to stop caffeine, when to eat, and when to seek or avoid light. The algorithm is based on the Two-Process Model of sleep regulation and NIOSH-validated circadian protocols — not an LLM, not a chatbot, not wellness advice generated by AI that's never worked a night shift.

**The story:**

An emergency medicine physician who lives the shift work problem builds the tool that doesn't exist. Not a sleep tracker (those tell you what happened). Not a meditation app (those assume you sleep at night). A circadian co-pilot that knows your schedule and tells you exactly what to do with your off-hours to protect your health.

**Why now:**

- The AHA published its first-ever Scientific Statement on Circadian Health in 2025, explicitly recommending the interventions ShiftWell automates
- 700 million people globally work shifts. 32% of US healthcare workers report short sleep. 10–38% of night shift workers develop a clinical sleep disorder.
- Nurse fatigue is a root cause in 23% of sentinel events (Joint Commission). Post-COVID healthcare staffing crisis has made this worse, not better.
- The $82B mHealth market has hundreds of sleep apps. Zero are built for shift workers.

**What makes this different from every other sleep app:**

- **Schedule-aware:** It doesn't assume you sleep at night. It reads your actual shifts and plans around them.
- **Deterministic algorithm:** No LLM hallucinations. The same schedule + chronotype = the same evidence-based plan every time.
- **Built by a shift worker:** I'm not a tech founder who read a paper about circadian rhythms. I work nights in an emergency department. The app exists because nothing else solved my problem.
- **Research-grade science:** Two-Process Model (Borbely 1982), NIOSH anchor sleep protocols, Eastman & Burgess light-shifting research, AHA 2025 circadian health recommendations. 1,059 automated tests ensure the algorithm doesn't drift.
