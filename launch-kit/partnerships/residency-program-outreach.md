That doesn't work. The data is ugly:

- **Residents averaging <6 hours of sleep** make **36% more serious medical errors** than those sleeping 7+ hours (Landrigan et al., NEJM 2004)
- **20% of residents** report falling asleep while driving home post-call (Barger et al., NEJM 2005)
- Sleep-deprived residents show cognitive impairment **equivalent to a BAC of 0.04–0.05%** (Dawson & Reid, Nature 1997)
- **Burnout rates exceed 50%** across residency programs, with sleep disruption as a primary driver (ACGME 2023 Survey)

ACGME duty hour rules cap work hours. They don't optimize sleep. A resident who works an 80-hour-compliant schedule can still be profoundly sleep-deprived if they don't manage their off-time circadian rhythm.

**What ShiftWell does:** The app imports a resident's rotation schedule and generates a personalized plan — optimal sleep windows, strategic pre-shift naps, caffeine cutoff times, light exposure protocols, and meal timing — all based on the Two-Process Model and NIOSH circadian science. It's deterministic (no LLM, no guesswork) and exports directly to their calendar.

**Why this matters for [PROGRAM_NAME]:**

- **ACGME wellness compliance:** The CLER Pathways and Common Program Requirements mandate fatigue mitigation education (VI.D). ShiftWell is a practical implementation, not a lecture.
- **Patient safety:** Fewer fatigued residents = fewer errors. Period.
- **Recruitment edge:** Gen Z and millennial residents choose programs that invest in their wellbeing. A free circadian optimization tool signals that [PROGRAM_NAME] takes wellness seriously beyond the checkbox.
- **Retention:** Burned-out residents leave. Sleep-protected residents stay.
