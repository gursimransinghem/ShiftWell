## Priority Targets by Type

### Hospitals (CNO Outreach)
Start with Tampa Bay area hospitals, then expand regionally. Prioritize facilities with known nurse retention challenges or recent Joint Commission surveys.

**Tier 1 — Local (warm leads, in-person possible):**
- HCA Florida Trinity Hospital (home facility — internal champion potential)
- Tampa General Hospital
- AdventHealth Tampa
- BayCare Health System (Morton Plant, St. Joseph's)
- Moffitt Cancer Center

**Tier 2 — Regional / National:**
- Mayo Clinic (Jacksonville)
- Cleveland Clinic Florida
- Large HCA facilities in other markets

### Residency Programs
Target EM first (natural fit), then surgery and OB/GYN (worst schedules).

**Tier 1:**
- USF Health Emergency Medicine Residency
- HCA Florida Bayonet Point EM Residency
- Local surgery and OB programs

**Tier 2:**
- National EM programs via CORD (Council of Residency Directors) network
- ACGME wellness officer contacts

### Wearables
WHOOP and Oura first. Garmin and Apple Watch later (broader but less differentiated).

### Media
Sequence: KevinMD guest post first (build credibility) → Becker's / Medscape → STAT / TechCrunch at launch.
