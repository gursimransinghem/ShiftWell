# ShiftWell Beta Feedback Surveys

Beta testers receive three structured surveys via email (Typeform or Google Forms) at Day 1, Week 1, and Week 4. These are designed for maximum completion by busy shift workers — short, clear, and actionable.

---

## Day 1 Survey: First Impressions & Setup Friction

**Sent:** 24 hours after app install  
**Estimated completion time:** 3–4 minutes  
**Goal:** Capture initial friction, setup clarity, trust signals, and shift worker demographics

### Survey Introduction
```
Hi! Thanks for downloading ShiftWell. We'd love to hear about your first 
24 hours with the app — this feedback helps us improve your experience.

This should take about 3 minutes.
```

### Questions

1. **How did you hear about ShiftWell?**
   - Type: Multiple choice (single select)
   - Options:
     - Friend/colleague recommendation
     - Social media or online ad
     - App Store search
     - Healthcare forum/community
     - Other: ___________

2. **How easy was it to set up ShiftWell?** (1 = very difficult, 5 = very easy)
   - Type: Scale (1–5)

3. **Which shift pattern best describes your work?**
   - Type: Multiple choice (single select)
   - Options:
     - 12-hour rotating (days/nights)
     - 8-hour fixed nights
     - 8-hour fixed days
     - 8-hour rotating (various patterns)
     - Irregular/on-call
     - Other: ___________

4. **How did you import your shift schedule?**
   - Type: Multiple choice (single select)
   - Options:
     - Manually entered shifts
     - Connected calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.)
     - Uploaded CSV/file
     - Haven't entered it yet

5. **Tell us about the chronotype quiz (the questions about your sleep habits).**
   - Type: Multiple choice (single select)
   - Options:
     - Clear and easy to understand
     - Somewhat confusing
     - Didn't do it yet
     - Not applicable

6. **What did you think of your first sleep plan recommendation?**
   - Type: Multiple choice (single select)
   - Options:
     - Realistic and helpful
     - Interesting but unclear how to use it
     - Unrealistic for my schedule
     - Haven't viewed it yet

7. **Did anything confuse you during setup?** (open-ended, optional)
   - Type: Open text
   - Placeholder: "If something wasn't clear, let us know..."

8. **Would you recommend ShiftWell to a colleague?**
   - Type: Multiple choice (single select)
   - Options:
     - Yes, definitely
     - Maybe, depends on [reason]
     - Not yet, need to use it longer
     - No, not interested

### Survey Closing
```
Thank you for getting ShiftWell off to a great start! Your feedback 
shapes every improvement we make.

We'll check back with you next week. Enjoy your personalized sleep plan.

— The ShiftWell Team
```

## Implementation Notes

### Typeform vs. Google Forms
- **Typeform:** Better UX for mobile (shift workers), progress bar, skip logic, prettier design, $35/month for unlimited responses
- **Google Forms:** Free, integrates with Google Workspace, simpler branching, good enough for surveys
- **Recommendation:** Use Typeform for Day 1 & Week 1; Google Forms acceptable for Week 4 (longer, lower completion pressure)

### Distribution
- **Channel:** Email (direct to beta tester inbox) + in-app prompt (optional link)
- **Subject line examples:**
  - Day 1: "Your first 24 hours with ShiftWell — 3 min feedback survey"
  - Week 1: "How's ShiftWell working for you? Week 1 check-in"
  - Week 4: "Help shape ShiftWell's future — final beta survey"
- **Send time:** Avoid 18:00–22:00 (end of shifts); aim for 09:00–12:00 or 14:00–16:00 local time

### Response Targets
- **Day 1:** 60–70% completion (high interest, fresh install)
- **Week 1:** 40–50% completion (attrition starts)
- **Week 4:** 25–35% completion (committed testers only, but higher quality feedback)

### Analysis & Follow-up
- **Day 1 results:** Quick hotfix prioritization (setup friction, obvious bugs)
- **Week 1 results:** Feature usage heatmap, early adoption patterns, quick wins
- **Week 4 results:** NPS analysis, pricing research, feature roadmap prioritization, cohort segmentation (by shift pattern, device, retention)
