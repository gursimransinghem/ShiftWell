# ShiftWell User Guide

## Welcome to ShiftWell

### What is ShiftWell?

ShiftWell is a scientifically-designed app that helps shift workers optimize their sleep and recovery. Instead of generic sleep advice, ShiftWell analyzes your unique shift schedule and generates personalized, day-by-day recommendations for sleep timing, naps, light exposure, caffeine, and meals—based on decades of peer-reviewed circadian science.

Whether you work nights, rotating shifts, or early mornings, ShiftWell adapts to your schedule and helps you:

- Sleep better despite difficult shift patterns
- Recover faster between work blocks
- Reduce fatigue and improve focus
- Protect your long-term health
- Export your sleep plan directly to your calendar

### Who is ShiftWell For?

ShiftWell is designed for:

- **Healthcare Workers** — nurses, doctors, paramedics, respiratory therapists, lab technicians
- **Emergency Services** — firefighters, police officers, 911 dispatchers
- **Manufacturing & Logistics** — warehouse workers, factory operators, truck drivers
- **Aviation & Transportation** — pilots, flight attendants, locomotive operators
- **Hospitality & Security** — hotel staff, security guards, night managers
- **Other Shift Work** — news broadcasters, power plant operators, military personnel, anyone working irregular hours

If you work a schedule where your sleep hours don't align with a typical 9-to-5 workday, ShiftWell is built for you.

## Getting Started

### Download & Install

ShiftWell is available for iOS. Search for "ShiftWell" in the App Store and tap **Install**.

### Create Your Account

When you open ShiftWell for the first time, you'll be prompted to create an account.

**Sign-in options:**
- **Apple Sign In** — Fast, private, uses your Apple account
- **Email & Password** — Create an account with your email address

Choose whichever is easiest for you. Both methods are equally secure and give you the same access to all features.

### Initial Onboarding Walkthrough

After signing in, ShiftWell will guide you through a quick setup:

**Step 1: Tell us about your sleep habits**
- When do you naturally wake up if you sleep unrestricted?
- How many hours of sleep do you typically need?
- Are you more of a morning person or night person? (chronotype)

This helps ShiftWell understand your natural circadian rhythm.

**Step 2: Describe your shift pattern**
- Do you work fixed shifts (e.g., always nights), rotating shifts, or variable shifts?
- How many hours are you typically scheduled per shift?
- How many days per week do you usually work?

ShiftWell uses this to predict your fatigue patterns.

**Step 3: Set your preferences**
- Do you want notifications? (for sleep reminders, nap windows, etc.)
- What's your timezone?
- Language preference (English or Spanish)

**Step 4: Import or add your first shift**
- Import from Google Calendar (auto-finds shifts), or
- Manually add a shift by date and time

After onboarding, you'll land on the **Today** tab with your first sleep plan ready to view.

## Setting Up Your Shift Schedule

ShiftWell's recommendations are only as good as your shift schedule. Keep your schedule accurate and up-to-date.

### Manual Shift Entry

To manually add a shift:

1. Tap the **Schedule** tab (calendar icon)
2. Tap **Add Shift** (+ button)
3. Enter:
   - **Date** — when does the shift occur?
   - **Start Time** — what time do you clock in?
   - **End Time** — when do you clock out?
   - **Recurring?** — does this repeat daily, weekly, or on specific days?
4. Tap **Save**

ShiftWell will immediately generate a sleep and recovery plan for that shift.

### Google Calendar Import

ShiftWell can auto-detect and import shifts from your Google Calendar, saving you manual entry.

**To set up Google Calendar import:**

1. Tap **Settings** (gear icon)
2. Tap **Connected Accounts**
3. Tap **Google Calendar**
4. Tap **Connect**
5. Follow the Google sign-in prompt and grant ShiftWell permission to read your calendar

Once connected, ShiftWell will:
- Scan your Google Calendar for shift-like events
- Import them as shifts in ShiftWell
- Sync any changes you make on either platform (within a few minutes)

**Important:** ShiftWell only reads your calendar. It does not modify or delete your events. You remain in full control of your Google Calendar.

### Recurring Shift Patterns

If you work a repeating pattern (e.g., 3 nights on, 4 days off), ShiftWell can learn this pattern:

1. Add 2–4 shifts from a typical cycle
2. ShiftWell's algorithm detects the pattern and auto-predicts future shifts
3. You'll see a **"Pattern Detected"** banner in the Schedule tab

You can still override any predicted shift by manually editing or deleting it.

### How the Algorithm Uses Your Schedule

ShiftWell's science-backed algorithm analyzes your shift schedule and generates:

- **Sleep Windows** — the optimal time to sleep given your upcoming shift
- **Nap Windows** — if sleep will be fragmented, ideal times for 20–90 min naps
- **Light Exposure Timing** — when to seek bright light and when to avoid it
- **Caffeine Cutoff** — the latest you should have caffeine to not disrupt sleep
- **Meal Timing** — when to eat to support your circadian rhythm and digestion

All recommendations are calculated using the Two-Process Model (Borbely 1982) and NIOSH protocols—the gold standard in sleep science. The algorithm is *deterministic*, not AI-based; it follows rigorous published research.

## Connecting WHOOP

### Why Connect WHOOP?

WHOOP measures Heart Rate Variability (HRV) and recovery—metrics that reveal how well your body is adapting to shift work. When you connect WHOOP to ShiftWell:

- ShiftWell sees your HRV and recovery data
- The algorithm adjusts recommendations based on *your actual recovery*, not just theory
- You get a personalized feedback loop: follow the plan → see better recovery → refine the plan

This is especially powerful for shift workers, where one person's ideal sleep window may differ from another's.

**You do not need WHOOP to use ShiftWell.** Disconnected users get great, science-backed plans. With WHOOP, you get personalization on top.

### How to Connect WHOOP

1. Make sure you have a WHOOP account and band (or app)
2. In ShiftWell, tap **Settings** → **Connected Accounts**
3. Tap **WHOOP**
4. Tap **Connect**
5. Sign in with your WHOOP credentials
6. Grant ShiftWell permission to read your HRV and recovery data
7. Tap **Done**

ShiftWell will sync WHOOP data daily.

### What Data is Synced?

- **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)** — a measure of nervous system stress/recovery
- **Recovery Score** — WHOOP's calculation of how recovered your body is (0–100%)
- **Sleep Duration** — how long you actually slept (from WHOOP)
- **Sleep Quality Metrics** — REM, deep sleep, disturbances

ShiftWell will **not** access:
- Your Strain score (workout data)
- Personal profile information
- Any data outside of recovery and sleep

### Privacy & Data Security

Your health data is encrypted end-to-end. ShiftWell stores only what's needed for personalized recommendations. You can disconnect WHOOP anytime without losing your ShiftWell history.

## Connecting Google Calendar

### Why Connect Google Calendar?

Google Calendar connect makes it easy to:

- **Auto-import shifts** — ShiftWell finds shift events in your calendar and imports them automatically
- **Export sleep plans** — ShiftWell can write your recommended sleep windows back to your Google Calendar so you see them in one place

This creates a single source of truth for your schedule and recovery.

**You do not need Google Calendar to use ShiftWell.** You can manually add shifts in ShiftWell and never touch Calendar.

### How to Connect Google Calendar

1. In ShiftWell, tap **Settings** → **Connected Accounts**
2. Tap **Google Calendar**
3. Tap **Connect**
4. You'll see a Google sign-in prompt
5. Sign in with the Google account that contains your shifts
6. You'll see a permissions screen. ShiftWell is requesting:
   - **Read access** to your calendar (to import shifts)
   - **Write access** to your calendar (to export sleep plans, optional)
7. Tap **Allow**
8. Return to ShiftWell—you're connected

### Permissions Explained

**Read Access** — ShiftWell can see events on your calendar. It looks for events with times that match typical shift patterns (e.g., "Night Shift 11 PM – 7 AM").

**Write Access** — ShiftWell can create events on your calendar for your sleep windows (e.g., "Sleep Window: 9 AM – 5 PM"). This is optional; you can disable it anytime.

**Important:** ShiftWell does not delete or modify any existing calendar events. It only adds new ones.

## Understanding Your Sleep Score

### What is a Sleep Score?

Your **Sleep Score** (0–100) is ShiftWell's assessment of your sleep quality and alignment with your circadian rhythm. It's not just about duration—it's about whether you're sleeping at the *right time* for your body.

A 90+ score means excellent sleep aligned with your rhythm. A 50 score might mean you slept 7 hours, but at a time when your body struggled to sleep.

### What Contributes to Your Score?

- **Sleep Duration** — Did you hit your personal sleep target? (e.g., 7 hours)
- **Sleep Timing** — Did you sleep during your optimal window? (determined by your circadian rhythm and shift)
- **Sleep Consistency** — Do you sleep at similar times each night? (your body prefers routine)
- **Circadian Alignment** — Is your sleep window positioned well relative to your upcoming shift?
- **Recovery** — If you use WHOOP, your HRV and recovery score are factored in

### How to Improve Your Score

1. **Follow the recommended sleep window** — ShiftWell calculates the optimal time to sleep for your shift. Sleeping outside that window, even if it's the same duration, will lower your score.

2. **Aim for consistency** — Sleep at roughly the same time each day, even on days off. Your circadian rhythm thrives on regularity.

3. **Respect your chronotype** — If ShiftWell says sleep at 6 AM and you're a morning person, that window should feel natural. If it feels wrong, tell ShiftWell your actual chronotype and it will recalibrate.

4. **Connect WHOOP** — HRV-based feedback makes your score more accurate and personalized.

5. **Sleep long enough** — Nailing the timing is only half the equation. Aim for the sleep duration you told ShiftWell you need.

Your score resets daily, so yesterday's low score doesn't matter—focus on tonight's sleep.

## Reading the Dashboard (Today Tab)

The **Today** tab is your command center. It shows your personalized plan for the current day and night.

### Today's Sleep Window

At the top, you'll see:
- **Sleep Window** — the time ShiftWell recommends you sleep (e.g., "9 AM – 5 PM")
- **Sleep Duration** — how long ShiftWell thinks you'll sleep (e.g., "8 hours")
- **Why This Window?** — a brief explanation (e.g., "Optimized for your 11 PM shift")

If you actually slept at a different time, tap **Edit Sleep** to log it. ShiftWell will recalculate your score.

### Upcoming Shift

Shows your next scheduled shift:
- **Date & Time** — when you work
- **Hours** — how long the shift is
- **Shift Type** — e.g., "Night Shift" (displayed in your timezone)

### Nap Windows (if applicable)

If your schedule doesn't allow a full sleep block, ShiftWell will suggest nap windows:
- **Nap 1** — e.g., "2 PM – 2:25 PM (20 min power nap)"
- **Nap 2** — e.g., "6 PM – 7:30 PM (90 min deep nap)"

Nap timing is science-backed. A 20-min nap hits just before deep sleep (refreshing but easy to wake). A 90-min nap gives you a full cycle (more restorative but groggier on waking).

### Caffeine Cutoff

Shows the latest time you should have caffeine:
- **Cutoff Time** — e.g., "2 PM"
- **Why?** — e.g., "Your shift is tonight; caffeine after 2 PM will interfere with your 9 AM sleep"

Caffeine has a half-life of ~5 hours, so ShiftWell works backward from your sleep window.

### Light Exposure Timing

- **Bright Light: 6 AM – 8 AM** — Get morning light (outside, or light therapy lamp) to anchor your circadian rhythm
- **Avoid Bright Light: 2 PM – 5 PM** — Bright light this afternoon will push your sleep later, delaying your sleep window
- **Blue Light (Phone/Screen): After 8 PM** — Avoid screens 1 hour before bed; use Night Sky Mode if you must use your phone

### Meal Timing

- **Breakfast: 7 AM** — Eat within 30 min of waking to reinforce your circadian rhythm
- **Dinner: 5:30 PM** — Eat 2–3 hours before your shift to optimize digestion and alertness
- **Avoid Heavy Meals: Within 2 Hours of Sleep** — Digestion interferes with sleep quality

All meal recommendations are based on your shift time and sleep window.

### Adaptive Feedback

ShiftWell learns from your behavior. If you ignore the nap recommendation and sleep 2 hours instead, ShiftWell will adjust tomorrow's plan based on what actually worked for you.

## The Circadian Tab

The **Circadian** tab visualizes your body's internal clock—the circadian rhythm that governs your sleep-wake cycle.

### Understanding the Curve

You'll see a smooth wave labeled:
- **Peak Alertness** — when your body naturally wants to be awake (usually midday)
- **Low Point** — when your body naturally wants to sleep (usually early morning)
- **Your Sleep Window** — highlighted in blue

The curve shifts based on:
- Your chronotype (whether you're naturally a morning or night person)
- Your shift schedule (your body adapts, but slowly)
- Light exposure (bright light advances or delays your rhythm)

### How Shifts Affect Your Rhythm

When you work night shifts, your circadian rhythm doesn't immediately flip. It takes 3–7 days to adapt. ShiftWell visualizes this:

- **Red zone** — Your rhythm is misaligned with your shift (expect poor sleep and alertness)
- **Yellow zone** — Your rhythm is partially adapted (some adjustment discomfort)
- **Green zone** — Your rhythm is well-aligned (optimal sleep and performance)

The algorithm predicts this adaptation over 30 days and adjusts your sleep window recommendations daily to guide your rhythm toward alignment.

### Using the Curve to Understand Fatigue

If you look at the Circadian tab and see your recommended sleep window falls during a "low alertness" period, that's why it's hard to sleep—your rhythm is fighting you. Stick with the plan anyway; the algorithm is moving your rhythm toward that window day by day.

## Weekly Briefs

Every Monday morning, ShiftWell generates an **AI-powered Weekly Brief** summarizing your sleep patterns, recovery, and recommendations for the week ahead.

### What's in a Weekly Brief?

- **Sleep Summary** — average sleep duration, best sleep, worst sleep
- **Recovery Trends** — if using WHOOP, your weekly HRV and recovery trajectory
- **Circadian Alignment** — percentage of nights you slept in the recommended window
- **Fatigue Forecast** — AI prediction of when you'll be most fatigued this week
- **Personalized Insight** — one actionable recommendation (e.g., "You recovered best when you napped on Tuesdays—consider scheduling a nap this Tuesday too")

### When Are They Generated?

Briefs are automatically generated every Monday at 6 AM your time. You'll get a notification (if you have notifications on) and can read it anytime in the **Brief** tab.

### How to Use Them

- **Share with your doctor** — Bring briefs to annual checkups or if you discuss your shift work with a physician
- **Adjust your schedule** — If the brief shows you're always exhausted on a certain day, talk to your manager about rotating that day
- **Validate the plan** — If the brief says you recovered best following a certain sleep pattern, that's your personalized optimal pattern

## Autopilot Mode

### What is Autopilot?

Autopilot is ShiftWell's 30-day self-optimization mode. When enabled, ShiftWell automatically:

- Adjusts your sleep window recommendations each day
- Tunes caffeine and light timing to your actual recovery
- Adapts meal recommendations based on your feedback
- Learns from your behavior and refines the plan

After 30 days, Autopilot "graduates" you with a personalized profile and hands over control to you. You can edit recommendations manually anytime.

### Who Should Use Autopilot?

- **New shift workers** — if you're new to night shifts or rotating schedules
- **Struggling with current routine** — if your current sleep plan isn't working
- **Changing shifts** — if your shift pattern changes, Autopilot can re-optimize

You do **not** need Autopilot if:
- Your current plan is working great
- You prefer full manual control
- Your schedule is very irregular (Autopilot works best with repeating patterns)

### How to Activate Autopilot

1. Tap **Settings** → **Preferences**
2. Toggle **Autopilot Mode: ON**
3. Tap **Start 30-Day Optimization**

For the next 30 days:
- You'll see a slight variation in your recommendations each day
- ShiftWell will ask for feedback: "Did you sleep well?" after each night
- You can override any recommendation, and Autopilot learns from that override

### After Autopilot Ends

On day 31, you'll see a summary of your learned preferences and the optimal sleep window ShiftWell calculated for you. You can:
- Accept the new recommendations and switch to manual mode
- Keep Autopilot running
- Revert to your original settings

## Customizing Notifications

ShiftWell can remind you at the right times. Customize which notifications you want and when.

### Types of Notifications

- **Sleep Reminder** — "Your sleep window starts in 30 min" (e.g., 8:30 AM if you sleep at 9 AM)
- **Nap Reminder** — "Nap window opens now" (only if you have naps scheduled)
- **Caffeine Cutoff Alert** — "No more caffeine after this time"
- **Light Exposure Reminder** — "Get bright light now" or "Avoid bright light now"
- **Weekly Brief Available** — "Your weekly brief is ready"
- **Sleep Feedback Request** — "How was your sleep last night?" (to improve scoring)

### How to Customize

1. Tap **Settings** (gear icon)
2. Tap **Notifications**
3. For each notification type, toggle **On/Off** and set a **time offset**:
   - **30 min before** — get a heads-up early
   - **At start time** — remind me when it starts
   - **1 hour before** — more lead time

Example:
- Sleep Window starts 9 AM → set Sleep Reminder to "30 min before" → you get notified at 8:30 AM

4. Tap **Save**

### Do Not Disturb Integration

ShiftWell respects your phone's Do Not Disturb (DND) settings:
- If DND is on during a notification time, ShiftWell will queue the notification and deliver it after DND ends
- You can exclude ShiftWell from DND (Settings → Notifications → Allow Critical Alerts) if you want shift reminders to break through DND

## FAQ

### Q1: Does ShiftWell work with rotating shifts?

**A:** Yes. ShiftWell is designed for rotating schedules. The algorithm adjusts your sleep recommendation daily based on your upcoming shift. If you work 3 nights, then 3 days off, then 3 evenings (a common pattern), ShiftWell adapts each day. Circadian adaptation takes 3–7 days, so the first few nights of a new shift may feel difficult; that's normal. Stick with the plan and your rhythm will adjust.

### Q2: Can I use ShiftWell without a WHOOP?

**A:** Absolutely. WHOOP is optional. Without it, ShiftWell generates recommendations using the gold-standard Two-Process Model and your shift schedule. You'll get great recommendations. With WHOOP, they become personalized based on *your* recovery. Either way, you benefit.

### Q3: How does the sleep algorithm work?

**A:** ShiftWell uses the Two-Process Model (published research from 1982) and NIOSH protocols. Your circadian rhythm naturally oscillates with a ~24-hour period. ShiftWell predicts where your rhythm peak (max alertness) and trough (max sleepiness) will be on any given night, then recommends you sleep during your predicted trough. The algorithm also factors in:
- Your chronotype (morning vs. night person)
- Your sleep pressure (how long you've been awake)
- Your shift schedule (when you work)
- Light exposure (which shifts your rhythm)
- Caffeine intake (which masks sleepiness)

It is fully deterministic—not AI-based—so results are reproducible and based on published research.

### Q4: Is my health data private?

**A:** Yes. All health data (shifts, sleep, WHOOP metrics, calendar) is encrypted in transit and at rest. ShiftWell does not sell your data. Your data is used only to generate personalized recommendations. You can delete your account anytime, which deletes all your data. For full details, see our Privacy Policy in Settings.

### Q5: Can I export my sleep plan to my calendar?

**A:** Yes, if you connect Google Calendar. Go to Settings → Connected Accounts → Google Calendar → Export Sleep Plans. ShiftWell will create calendar events for each sleep window so you see them alongside your shifts and other commitments. You can disable export anytime.

### Q6: Does it work for 12-hour shifts?

**A:** Yes. ShiftWell adapts to any shift length (8, 10, 12, or 24 hours). Longer shifts change when you can sleep, but the algorithm accounts for this. A 12-hour night shift (e.g., 7 PM – 7 AM) will give you a sleep recommendation around those hours. If you work back-to-back 12-hour shifts, ShiftWell will recommend a short nap between them.

### Q7: What if I can't follow the recommended sleep window?

**A:** ShiftWell adapts. If you sleep at a different time, open the **Sleep** card on the Today tab and tap **Edit Sleep** to log your actual sleep. The algorithm will:
1. Recalculate your sleep score based on what actually happened
2. Adjust tomorrow's recommendation based on how you recovered

Over time, ShiftWell learns your constraints and personal adaptation, and recommendations become more realistic for your life.

### Q8: How is this different from Sleep Cycle / Oura / other sleep apps?

**A:** 
- **Sleep Cycle** tracks sleep but doesn't optimize for shift work or circadian misalignment.
- **Oura** measures sleep quality but doesn't generate shift-optimized recommendations.
- **Generic fitness apps** assume you sleep at night.
- **ShiftWell** is purpose-built for shift workers. It predicts your circadian rhythm *in advance* and generates a personalized, science-backed plan *before* you sleep, not after. It factors in your shift schedule, caffeine timing, light exposure, and recovery data—everything that affects sleep quality for shift workers.

### Q9: Can I use it in Spanish?

**A:** Yes. Go to Settings → Language and select **Español**. The entire app—all screens, notifications, briefs, recommendations—switches to Spanish. Your data and plans remain the same across language switches.

### Q10: What happens after the free trial?

**A:** If you start a 7-day premium trial and don't cancel by day 7, your subscription will be charged $29.99 (annual). You'll then have unlimited Autopilot, weekly briefs, fatigue forecasting, and other premium features. You can cancel anytime in iOS Settings → App Store → Subscriptions. If you cancel, you lose premium features but keep your free account (all your shifts, sleep data, and basic recommendations).

## Troubleshooting

### Calendar Not Syncing

**Problem:** I connected Google Calendar but my shifts aren't showing up in ShiftWell.

**Solution:**
1. Make sure your shift events in Google Calendar have a clear time (not all-day events)
2. Make sure the calendar you shared is the same one ShiftWell has permission to read
3. Go to Settings → Connected Accounts → Google Calendar → Tap "..." → Re-Sync
4. Wait 2–3 minutes and refresh ShiftWell

If still not working:
1. Tap Settings → Connected Accounts → Google Calendar → Disconnect
2. Reconnect and grant permissions again
3. Manually add one shift to test; if it works, try syncing again

### WHOOP Not Connecting

**Problem:** I can't connect WHOOP or it disconnects randomly.

**Solution:**
1. Make sure you're signed into WHOOP on your phone
2. Make sure ShiftWell has permission to access WHOOP (go to WHOOP app → Settings → Connected Apps → ShiftWell → Allow)
3. Restart ShiftWell
4. If that doesn't work: Settings → Connected Accounts → WHOOP → Disconnect, then reconnect

If WHOOP keeps disconnecting:
- This usually means your WHOOP credentials expired. Sign out of WHOOP, sign back in, and reconnect to ShiftWell.

### Notifications Not Appearing

**Problem:** I'm not getting notifications even though I turned them on.

**Solution:**
1. Go to iOS Settings → Notifications → ShiftWell → Allow Notifications (toggle ON)
2. Make sure "Time Sensitive" or "Critical Alerts" is enabled if you want reminders to break through Do Not Disturb
3. Check your notification schedule: Settings → Notifications in ShiftWell and verify times are set correctly
4. Restart your phone

### Sleep Score Seems Wrong

**Problem:** I slept 8 hours but my score is 50—what's wrong?

**Solution:**
Your score reflects sleep *quality and timing*, not just duration. A score of 50 usually means:
- You slept at a suboptimal time for your rhythm (e.g., you slept 9 AM – 5 PM but ShiftWell predicted your trough is 10 AM – 6 AM)
- Your sleep was inconsistent (went to bed at 9:30 AM one day, 8 AM another)
- You have poor HRV or recovery data (if using WHOOP)

To improve:
1. Try sleeping in the recommended window (follow the blue highlighted time on the Today tab)
2. Log your actual sleep time accurately (tap Edit Sleep)
3. Maintain consistent sleep times—even on days off
4. Connect WHOOP for personalized adjustments

### App Crashing

**Problem:** The app keeps crashing when I open a specific tab.

**Solution:**
1. Force-close ShiftWell: swipe up from the bottom of your screen (or use the app switcher) and swipe the ShiftWell card up
2. Reopen ShiftWell
3. Try the troublesome tab again
4. If it crashes again, try clearing app data: iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → ShiftWell → Offload App → Reinstall App

This removes cached data but keeps your account and all your history.

### Still Having Issues?

If you've tried the above and still have problems:
1. Go to **Settings → Support → Contact Us**
2. Describe your issue and include a screenshot if possible
3. Our support team will get back to you within 24 hours

For premium subscribers, support is prioritized.
