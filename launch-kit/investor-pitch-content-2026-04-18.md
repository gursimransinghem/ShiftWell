## Slide 3: The Solution

**Headline:** ShiftWell Doesn't Track Your Sleep. It Optimizes It.

**Content:**

- Reads your actual work calendar (iCal, QGenda, Google Calendar) and personal calendar
- Applies the Two-Process Model of sleep regulation (Borbely 1982) personalized to your chronotype and shift pattern
- Generates a complete circadian plan: sleep windows, strategic naps, caffeine cutoffs, meal timing, light protocols
- Exports the plan back to your calendar as real, color-coded events
- Deterministic algorithm — not an LLM. Same inputs = same outputs. Auditable, explainable, traceable to published research.

**Tagline:** *"Every other app tells you how you slept. ShiftWell tells you how to sleep."*

**Speaker Notes:**
"The key insight is this: shift workers don't need another sleep tracker. They need a sleep planner. ShiftWell reads your work schedule from your calendar, reads your personal calendar so it knows you have a dentist appointment at 7 AM after a night shift, and generates a personalized circadian optimization plan covering five protocols — sleep, naps, caffeine, meals, and light. Then it exports that plan right back to your calendar. The algorithm is deterministic — based on the Two-Process Model, which has been the gold standard in sleep science since 1982. Same schedule in, same plan out. No hallucinations, no black box. Every recommendation traces to a published paper. That matters for enterprise buyers who need to explain what they're giving their staff."

**Data Sources:**
- Borbely (1982) A Two-Process Model of Sleep Regulation — Human Neurobiology

## Slide 4: Demo / Product

**Headline:** From Schedule to Sleep Plan in 60 Seconds

**Content — Key Screens Flow:**

1. **Schedule Input** — Import shifts from calendar or enter manually. App detects shift pattern (fixed nights, rotating, 12s, irregular).
2. **Personalized Sleep Plan** — Full week view with color-coded blocks: primary sleep (blue), anchor sleep (navy), naps (teal), caffeine cutoff (amber), meals (green), light protocol (yellow).
3. **Today Screen** — At-a-glance: countdown to next sleep window, caffeine cutoff timer, next shift, daily adherence score. One-tap calendar export.
4. **Substance Tracking** — Log caffeine and alcohol intake. App adjusts next-day plan based on pharmacokinetics (caffeine half-life: 5–6 hours, Burke et al. 2015).
5. **Fatigue Alerts** — When your plan shows <6 hours of sleep opportunity before a shift, you get a proactive warning with recovery recommendations.

**Speaker Notes:**
"Let me walk you through the user experience. You connect your calendar — takes about 30 seconds. ShiftWell reads your shifts and personal events, identifies your shift pattern, and generates a full week of circadian-optimized recommendations. The Today screen is the daily dashboard — it shows your next sleep window, when to stop caffeine, when your next shift starts, and your adherence score. If you log a coffee at 4 PM before a night shift, the algorithm adjusts your next sleep plan based on caffeine pharmacokinetics. If your schedule shows less than 6 hours of sleep opportunity before a shift, you get a fatigue alert with specific recovery tactics. Everything exports to your calendar with one tap."
