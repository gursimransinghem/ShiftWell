## One-Pager: What ShiftWell Is

**ShiftWell** is an iOS app that turns a shift worker's calendar into a science-backed sleep plan.

The app imports work schedules from Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or QGenda. It reads personal calendar events. Then it generates a complete circadian optimization plan — sleep windows, strategic naps, caffeine cutoffs, meal timing, and light exposure protocols — and exports the plan directly back to the user's calendar as real, color-coded events.

The algorithm is built on the Two-Process Model of sleep regulation (Borbely, 1982), NIOSH anchor sleep protocols, and recommendations from the American Heart Association's 2025 Scientific Statement on circadian disruption. It is deterministic — no LLM inference, no hallucinated wellness advice. Every recommendation traces to peer-reviewed research.

**Who it's for:** Nurses, physicians, paramedics, firefighters, police officers, pilots, factory workers — anyone whose work schedule conflicts with their body clock. 700 million shift workers worldwide. 22 million in the United States. 32% of US healthcare workers report short sleep (NIOSH).

**Why now:** The AHA's 2025 Scientific Statement elevated circadian health to mainstream awareness. Post-COVID healthcare worker burnout has created acute demand for fatigue solutions. No existing app combines calendar integration with circadian science for shift workers. The technology gap is wide open.

**Business model:** Freemium with a $29.99/year annual subscription (7-day premium trial). No hardware required — works on iPhone alone.

**Status:** Feature-complete MVP with 1,059 automated tests passing. Pre-TestFlight. iOS-first launch planned Q2 2026.

## Key Stats & Talking Points

**The Problem:**
- 700 million shift workers globally (ILO)
- 22 million shift workers in the United States (BLS)
- 32% of US healthcare workers report short sleep (NIOSH)
- 10–38% of night shift workers develop Shift Work Sleep Disorder (Drake et al., 2004)
- 23% of sentinel events in hospitals attributed to nurse fatigue (Joint Commission)
- $136.4 billion annual cost of fatigue-related productivity loss (Rosekind et al., 2010)
- Night shift workers file 2–3x more workers' compensation claims (NSC)
- 27.65% annual nurse turnover rate nationally

**The Solution:**
- Only app that imports shift schedule AND exports sleep plan to the same calendar
- Reads personal calendar events to adjust plans around real obligations
- Based on 15+ peer-reviewed studies (not wellness blog advice)
- Implements every intervention recommended by the AHA's 2025 Scientific Statement
- No wearable or hardware required — iPhone only
- $29.99/year — fraction of the cost of WHOOP ($300+/yr), Oura ($420+/yr), or Rise ($70/yr)

**The Founder Angle:**
- Built by an ER physician who works the shifts this app optimizes
- Solo-built: algorithm, UI, 1,059 tests, business plan
- Self-taught coder — learned React Native to solve his own problem
- Independent project — not corporate, not VC-funded, not hospital-affiliated
