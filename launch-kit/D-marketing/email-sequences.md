## Sequence 4: Day 3 Follow-Up

**Subject:** How's your first plan working?
**Preview text:** Quick check-in — 3 days in.

---

Hey —

You've had ShiftWell for 3 days. Quick check-in.

**If your plan is working:** That's the goal. The algorithm is doing its job. You shouldn't have to think about when to sleep — just follow the calendar blocks.

**If something feels off:** That's useful feedback. A few common things in the first week:

*"The sleep window feels too early/late"*
→ Check your chronotype setting in the app. Morning larks and night owls get different windows. If it still feels wrong, ShiftWell adapts as you use it.

*"I can't actually sleep during the suggested window"*
→ Normal for the first 2-3 days, especially if you're shifting from a stretch of nights. The plan accounts for circadian lag. Give it a few days before adjusting.

*"I didn't follow the plan because [life happened]"*
→ That's fine. ShiftWell regenerates when your calendar changes. Missed a window? The next plan adapts. No guilt. No streaks to break.

One thing to try this week: follow the caffeine cutoff time. It's based on Drake et al. (2013) — caffeine has a 6-hour half-life. Most people are surprised how much this one change improves their sleep onset.

If you have questions or feedback, just reply. I read everything.

— Sim

You'll see this as a recurring block that stays fixed while other windows flex.
