**Post 3: Anchor Sleep — The One Rule That Changes Everything**
📸 Visual: Split-screen showing "anchor sleep" window consistent across day/night shift weeks
Caption: NIOSH (the CDC's occupational health division) recommends keeping at least 4 hours of sleep at the same time every day — even when you switch between days and nights. They call it anchor sleep. It stabilizes your circadian clock during transitions. ShiftWell protects your anchor sleep window and builds your full plan around it. One rule. Massive impact.
#anchorsleep #NIOSH #shiftwork #circadianrhythm #nightshift #sleeptips #nursesofinstagram #shiftworker #sleepscience #rotatingshifts

**Post 4: The Math of Sleep Debt**
📸 Visual: Running tally showing sleep debt accumulating over a 3-day stretch of night shifts
Caption: Night shift Monday: -1.5 hours. Tuesday: -2 hours. Wednesday: -1 hour. By Thursday you're carrying 4.5 hours of sleep debt. That's the equivalent of being legally drunk in terms of cognitive impairment (Williamson & Feyer, 2000). ShiftWell tracks your sleep debt and adjusts recovery sleep and naps to pay it back.
#sleepdebt #nightshift #shiftwork #patientsafety #nurselife #fatigue #sleepscience #cognitiveimpairment #healthcare #shiftworker

**Post 5: Your Body Has Two Clocks**
📸 Visual: Diagram showing Process S (sleep pressure) and Process C (circadian) from the Two-Process Model
Caption: Sleep isn't one system — it's two. Process S builds pressure the longer you're awake. Process C is your 24-hour circadian clock. When they align, you sleep easily. When they fight (hello, night shift), you lie in bed staring at the ceiling. The Two-Process Model (Borbely, 1982) is the foundation of sleep science. It's also the foundation of ShiftWell's algorithm.
#twoprocessmodel #sleepscience #circadianrhythm #nightshift #shiftwork #processS #processC #borbely #sleepresearch #nursesofinstagram

**Post 6: The AHA Finally Said It**

Caption: The American Heart Association published a 2025 Scientific Statement saying circadian disruption increases cardiovascular risk — and recommending timed sleep, light protocols, and meal timing as countermeasures. **Post 7: Time-Restricted Eating for Night Shift**
