## Implementation Notes

### Technical Setup
- **ESP:** Resend (recommended for dev-friendly API) or SendGrid
- **Trigger:** Webhook on user signup → start drip sequence
- **Personalization tokens:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{sleep_score}}`, `{{shifts_logged}}`, `{{days_active}}`, `{{plan_type}}`
- **Dynamic content:** Email 7 splits on `{{user_type}}` (beta vs. paid trial)
- **Suppression:** Skip Email 3 if user has logged sleep 5+ times (they're already engaged — send an advanced tip instead)
- **Unsubscribe:** One-click, in footer of every email. Required by CAN-SPAM.

### A/B Testing Protocol
- Test subject lines first (highest-leverage variable for open rates)
- Run each test for minimum 100 sends before declaring a winner
- Track: open rate, click rate, app open within 24h of email
- Winner = highest click rate (not open rate — clicks indicate intent)

### Key Metrics to Track
| Metric | Target | Measured At |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Open rate (avg across sequence) | >45% | All emails |
| Click rate (avg) | >8% | All emails |
| Calendar connection rate | >60% | After Email 4 |
| NPS survey completion | >30% | After Email 5 |
| 30-day retention | >40% | After Email 7 |
| Trial → paid conversion | >25% | After Email 7B |

### Compliance
- CAN-SPAM compliant (physical address in footer, one-click unsubscribe)
- No health claims that imply diagnosis or treatment
- Citations in Email 6 are real, published studies — verify URLs before sending
- HIPAA: no personal health data in email. Sleep scores referenced are prompts to check the app, not transmitted via email.
