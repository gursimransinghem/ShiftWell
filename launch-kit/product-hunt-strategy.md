# ShiftWell Product Hunt Launch Strategy

## Executive Summary

Product Hunt remains one of the highest-leverage launch channels for consumer health apps. This document is a complete playbook for ShiftWell's PH launch — from 4 weeks of pre-launch groundwork through launch day execution and post-launch conversion. The strategy is calibrated for the post-2024 algorithm reality where only ~10% of submissions get featured, and featuring accounts for ~70% of success.

ShiftWell has a significant narrative advantage: a practicing ER physician who built a sleep optimization app between overnight shifts. That founder story is PH gold — lean into it hard.

---

## 1. Pre-Launch Phase (Weeks 1-4 Before Launch)

### Week 4: Foundation

**Create Your Upcoming Page**

Product Hunt lets you create an "Upcoming" page up to a month before launch. Do this immediately.

- **Tagline (60 char max):** "Science-backed sleep plans for shift workers" (45 chars)
  - Alternatives: "Your shift schedule, optimized for sleep" (40 chars) | "Circadian AI for the 700M who work nights" (42 chars)
- **Description (500 char max):**

> ShiftWell reads your shift schedule and builds a personalized sleep, nap, caffeine, light exposure, and meal timing plan — backed by the Two-Process Model, AASM guidelines, and NIOSH protocols. Built by an ER physician who works nights. No wearable required. Import your calendar, get a science-backed plan, export it back. Free core features, $29.99/yr for premium.

- **Subscribe button:** Active from day one. Goal: 500+ subscribers before launch day.
- **Tags:** Sleep, Health & Fitness, Productivity (max 3)

**Maker Profile Optimization**

Your Product Hunt profile is your credibility. Set it up properly:

- Profile photo: Professional headshot (scrubs or white coat — you're a doctor, use it)
- Bio: "Emergency Medicine Physician (DO) | Building ShiftWell — circadian optimization for shift workers | Tampa, FL"
- Link your Twitter/X and personal site
- Add "Maker" badge by listing ShiftWell as your product
- Follow relevant makers in health/wellness space

### Week 3: Hunter Strategy

**Self-Hunt vs. Top Hunter — The Verdict: Self-Hunt**

Product Hunt significantly reduced the weight of top hunters in 2024-2025. Follower notifications from hunters were eliminated years ago. Self-hunted products now account for ~60% of successful launches.

| Factor | Self-Hunt | Top Hunter |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| Algorithm weight | Equal since 2024 | No longer provides boost |
| Authenticity | You tell your own story | Hunter may not know your product deeply |
| Control | Full control of timing, copy, assets | Depends on hunter's schedule |
| First comment | You write it naturally as maker | Separate coordination needed |
| Cost | Free | Some hunters charge $500-$2,000+ |
| Risk | None | Hunter may delay, deprioritize, or launch poorly |

**Recommendation: Self-hunt.** Your founder story IS the product's differentiator on PH. An ER doc launching their own app is more compelling than a professional hunter posting it. Save the money for App Store ads.

### Week 2-3: Community Engagement (Build Karma)

Product Hunt's algorithm favors accounts with genuine community participation. This is not optional.

**Daily actions (15 min/day for 2-3 weeks):**
- Upvote 3-5 products you genuinely find interesting
- Leave 2-3 thoughtful comments on new launches (not "Great product!" — add substance)
- Focus on health, wellness, productivity, and developer tool launches
- Reply to comments on products you've engaged with
- Follow 5-10 makers per day in adjacent spaces

**What this accomplishes:**
- Your account looks established, not freshly created for a launch
- You build genuine connections who may support your launch
- PH's algorithm weights upvotes from established accounts higher
- You learn what works on PH by observing successful launches

### Week 1: Final Prep

**Build Your Launch Support List**

Compile a spreadsheet of people who will support launch day. Aim for 200+ people in your network. Categories:

- **Tier 1 (Inner circle, 20-30 people):** Friends, family, colleagues who will upvote + comment within the first hour. Brief them individually. They need PH accounts created NOW, not on launch day (new accounts are flagged).
- **Tier 2 (Professional network, 50-100 people):** Fellow physicians, medical Twitter contacts, health tech people. Send a personal heads-up email.
- **Tier 3 (Broader community, 100+):** Email list subscribers, social media followers, beta testers. Mass notification on launch day.

**Critical rule:** NEVER send a direct link to your PH page with "please upvote." This triggers PH's anti-manipulation detection. Instead, say: "We launched on Product Hunt today — would love your feedback!" and link to producthunt.com (the homepage), where they can find you organically.

**Social Media Tease Schedule:**
- Day -7: "Something I've been building for the past year is almost ready to share..."
- Day -5: Behind-the-scenes screenshot of the app
- Day -3: "Launching on Product Hunt this [day]. Here's why I built it..." (short thread)
- Day -1: "Tomorrow. 12:01 AM Pacific. Product Hunt." with app preview image

## 2. Launch Day Playbook

### Optimal Launch Day and Time

**When:** Tuesday or Wednesday at 12:01 AM Pacific Time

Tuesday and Wednesday consistently outperform other days. The PH daily cycle resets at midnight PT. Launching at 12:01 AM gives you a full 24-hour window to accumulate upvotes before the day ends.

Avoid: Monday (crowded with weekend-prepped launches), Friday (low engagement into weekend), weekends (lowest traffic).

**For you specifically:** If you're working a night shift, this actually works perfectly — you're already awake at 3:01 AM ET. If you're on days, set an alarm or schedule everything in advance.

### Hour-by-Hour Execution

**11:30 PM PT (Night Before)**
- Final check: all assets uploaded, links working, description proofread
- Draft your first comment (see below) — have it ready to paste
- Alert Tier 1 supporters: "Going live in 30 minutes"
- Queue social media posts for auto-publish at 12:05 AM PT

**12:01 AM PT — LAUNCH**
- Submit the product (or it auto-publishes if pre-scheduled)
- Immediately post your first comment (within 2 minutes)
- Verify everything renders correctly on the live page
- Text/DM your Tier 1 supporters: "We're live!"

**12:01 - 1:00 AM PT (Golden Hour)**
- Tier 1 supporters upvote and leave genuine comments
- You respond to every single comment within 15 minutes
- Target: 30-50 upvotes + 10-15 comments in the first hour
- This early velocity signals to PH's algorithm that your launch is worth featuring

**6:00 - 8:00 AM PT (West Coast Wakes Up)**
- Post on Twitter/X with screenshot of PH page
- Post on LinkedIn (different angle — professional/founder story)
- Send email blast to Tier 2 supporters
- Continue responding to every comment

**9:00 AM - 12:00 PM PT (Peak Traffic)**
- This is when PH traffic peaks. Your ranking is largely set by now.
- Post on relevant subreddits (r/shiftwork, r/nursing, r/sleep, r/medicine — check each sub's self-promo rules)
- Send to Tier 3 / broader email list
- Cross-post to Instagram Stories
- Monitor and respond to all comments — engagement rate matters more than upvote count

**12:00 - 6:00 PM PT (Sustain)**
- Continue responding to comments (1 quality comment from you ≈ 40-50 upvotes in algorithm weight)
- Share any milestones on social: "We're #3 on Product Hunt right now!"
- Post afternoon social media reminder
- DM anyone who commented to thank them personally

**6:00 PM - 12:00 AM PT (Final Push)**
- Last social media push
- Thank supporters publicly
- Start drafting your "How I Built ShiftWell" blog post while energy is high

### First Comment Strategy (The Maker's Story)

The first comment is the most important piece of copy after your tagline. It's where you humanize the product. PH users read it before deciding to upvote.

**Draft First Comment:**

> Hey Product Hunt! I'm Sim, an emergency medicine physician in Tampa, FL. I work rotating day/night shifts in the ER, and I built ShiftWell because I was tired of being... well, tired.
>
> Here's the reality: 700 million people worldwide work shifts. 32% of US healthcare workers report short sleep. I'm one of them. After years of flipping between day and night shifts and feeling wrecked, I started digging into the circadian science research — Two-Process Model, AASM guidelines, NIOSH anchor sleep protocols — and realized the knowledge exists to optimize shift worker sleep. It's just buried in academic papers that nobody has time to read at 3 AM.
>
> So I built ShiftWell. You import your shift schedule, it reads your personal calendar, and it generates a complete sleep, nap, caffeine cutoff, light exposure, and meal timing plan — all backed by published research. No wearable required. No generic "sleep hygiene tips." Just a plan built around YOUR actual schedule.
>
> What makes this different from sleep trackers:
> - It's **proactive** (plans ahead) not **reactive** (tells you last night sucked)
> - It knows your shift pattern and adapts to day↔night transitions
> - The algorithm is deterministic and science-based, not an LLM hallucinating sleep advice
> - It exports directly to your calendar
>
> I built most of this between patients on night shifts (the irony is not lost on me).
>
> Free to use with core features. Premium is $29.99/year for advanced circadian shifting plans, multi-week optimization, and calendar sync.
>
> I'd love your feedback — especially from fellow shift workers, healthcare folks, or anyone who's struggled with irregular sleep. What would make this more useful for you?

**Why this works:**
- Personal story creates emotional connection
- Specific numbers (700M, 32%) establish credibility
- "Built between patients at 3 AM" is memorable and shareable
- Ends with a question to drive comment engagement
- Mentions pricing transparently (PH community respects this)

### Response Templates for Common Questions/Comments

**"How is this different from [Rise/Sleep Cycle/etc]?"**
> Great question! Most sleep apps are trackers — they tell you how you slept last night. ShiftWell is a planner — it looks at your upcoming shifts and builds a forward-looking plan. Rise is excellent for sleep debt tracking, but it doesn't know you're switching from days to nights next week and need a circadian transition protocol. That shift-aware planning is our core differentiator.

**"Does it work with [Apple Watch/Oura/etc]?"**
> We integrate with Apple Health, so any wearable that writes to HealthKit feeds into ShiftWell. But here's the thing — you don't need a wearable at all. The algorithm runs on your schedule, not your biometrics. Wearable data is a nice-to-have for validation, not a requirement.

**"I'm not a shift worker, would this help me?"**
> If you have any kind of irregular schedule — travel across time zones, on-call rotations, early morning starts followed by late nights — yes. The circadian optimization engine works for anyone whose sleep timing isn't the same every day. We're expanding to travelers, new parents, and students post-launch.

**"Is the science actually legit?"**
> Every recommendation traces to published, peer-reviewed research. The core algorithm is based on Borbely's Two-Process Model (1982), AASM sleep guidelines (2015/2023), Eastman & Burgess circadian shifting protocols (2009), and NIOSH anchor sleep recommendations. I'm a practicing physician — I wouldn't put my name on pseudoscience. Happy to share our full citation list.

**"What about HIPAA / privacy?"**
> ShiftWell processes your schedule data locally on-device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any health data to external servers. No account required for core features. Full privacy policy is on our website. Your sleep schedule is yours.

### Upvote Ethics: What's Allowed vs. What Gets You Flagged

**Allowed:**
- Asking your network to check out your PH launch
- Sharing on social media and email
- Responding to comments to drive engagement
- Offering a launch-day promo code or discount

**Will get you flagged/penalized:**
- Sending direct links with "please upvote" language
- Upvote-exchange groups/pods (PH actively detects these)
- Paying for upvotes or using upvote services
- Creating fake accounts to upvote
- Mass-DMing PH users you don't know
- Sudden spikes of upvotes from new/inactive accounts
- Coordinated upvoting from the same IP address or location

**The safest approach:** Link to producthunt.com (homepage) not your product page directly. Say "we launched today, would love your thoughts" — never "please upvote."

## 3. Assets Needed

### Thumbnail / Logo

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Dimensions | 240 x 240 px (square) |
| File size | Under 3 MB |
| Format | PNG recommended; GIF allowed (hover-to-animate) |
| Content | App icon on solid background — clean, recognizable at small size |
| Note | If using GIF, first frame = static thumbnail (no autoplay) |

**Recommendation:** Use the ShiftWell app icon on a dark background (dark-mode-first brand). A subtle animated GIF showing a day/night cycle transition would be eye-catching but the static fallback frame must look good standalone.

### Gallery Images (Minimum 2, Recommend 5-6)

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Dimensions | 1270 x 760 px |
| File size | Under 3 MB each |
| Format | PNG or GIF |

**Recommended gallery order (storytelling sequence):**

1. **Hero shot:** App on iPhone mockup with headline "Sleep Plans Built Around Your Shifts" — dark mode UI, showing the main schedule view
2. **The problem:** Split screen — shift worker's chaotic schedule on left, ShiftWell's organized plan on right. Text: "700M shift workers. Zero apps built for them."
3. **How it works:** 3-step flow — "Import Schedule → Get Your Plan → Export to Calendar"
4. **Science credibility:** Screenshot of the circadian optimization view with callouts to research sources. Text: "Built on peer-reviewed science, not sleep hygiene clichés"
5. **Feature highlights:** Grid showing key features — nap windows, caffeine cutoffs, light exposure timing, meal scheduling
6. **Social proof / founder:** Photo of you in scrubs + quote about why you built it. This humanizes the product and reinforces the ER doc angle.

### Demo Video

| Spec | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Length | 60 seconds max (aim for 45-60) |
| Host | YouTube (cannot be private, unlisted is OK) |
| Format | Screen recording of the app, not a talking-head pitch |

**53% of products that reached Product of the Day since 2021 included a video.** Don't skip this.

**60-Second Video Script:**

```
[0-5s] Text on screen: "You work shifts. Your sleep app doesn't know that."
[5-12s] Show a rotating shift schedule (Meditech/QGenda style) — chaotic, 
        alternating days and nights
[12-18s] ShiftWell import: schedule flows into the app. Text: "Import your shifts"
[18-30s] Algorithm generates plan: sleep windows, nap times, caffeine cutoffs, 
        light exposure all populate on a timeline. Show the transition plan for 
        a day-to-night shift change.
[30-40s] Quick feature montage: circadian phase diagram, meal timing, 
        calendar export
[40-48s] Calendar export: plan items appear in Google Calendar
[48-55s] Text: "Built by an ER physician. Backed by peer-reviewed science."
[55-60s] ShiftWell logo + "Free on the App Store" + Product Hunt badge
```

**Production tips:**
- Record on actual device, not simulator (feels more real)
- Dark mode throughout (matches brand)
- Background music: subtle, low-energy ambient (the irony of energetic music for a sleep app won't land well)
- No voiceover needed — text overlays + screen recording is the PH-native format
- Use a tool like Screen Studio (Mac) for smooth device-frame recordings

### Animated GIF of Key Feature

- **What to animate:** The "schedule import → plan generation" flow. Show a shift schedule being imported and the sleep plan populating in real-time. 5-8 seconds, looping.
- **Dimensions:** 1270 x 760 (gallery size) for PH; also create a 600 x 400 version for social sharing.
- **File size:** Keep under 3 MB (optimize with gifski or similar)

## 4. Post-Launch (Days 1-14)

### Day 1-2: Immediate Follow-Up

- **Respond to every remaining comment** on your PH page. Response rate is visible and matters for social proof.
- **Thank supporters publicly** on Twitter/X and LinkedIn. Tag people who left great comments.
- **Screenshot your results** — final ranking, upvote count, any badges earned. You'll use these forever.
- **Collect testimonials** from commenters who said positive things. Ask permission to quote them.

### Day 2-5: Founder Story Blog Post

**"How I Built ShiftWell: From Night Shifts in the ER to the App Store"**

Publish on Medium, your personal site, and cross-post to dev.to / Indie Hackers.

Outline:
- The problem: Your personal experience with shift work sleep deprivation
- The research rabbit hole: Discovering the Two-Process Model, AASM guidelines, NIOSH protocols
- The build: Learning to code as a physician, building between shifts (real timeline, real struggles)
- The science: How you translated academic papers into an algorithm
- The launch: Product Hunt day experience, what surprised you
- What's next: Roadmap, vision for shift workers globally

**Why this matters:** PH traffic spikes on launch day but the founder story has a long tail. It gets shared in newsletters, picked up by health tech blogs, and surfaces in Google searches for months.

### Day 3-7: Leverage the PH Badge

- **Website:** Add "Featured on Product Hunt" badge to your landing page (PH provides embed code)
- **App Store:** Add "Featured on Product Hunt" to your app description and screenshot set
- **Email signature:** Add PH badge for the next 30 days
- **Social profiles:** Update bios to include "Featured on Product Hunt"

### Day 7-14: Convert PH Traffic

PH traffic drops off after 48 hours but trickles for weeks. Make sure your funnel is ready:

- **Landing page:** PH-specific landing page or UTM-tagged link that acknowledges "Welcome from Product Hunt!" and offers a launch-week promo
- **Email capture:** Anyone who visits but doesn't download immediately should hit an email signup
- **Retargeting pixel:** Have it live on your site before launch day
- **App Store Optimization:** Ensure your App Store listing is polished before PH launch — PH sends traffic directly to the store

## 5. Realistic Expectations

### What Does a "Good" PH Launch Look Like for a Health App?

Health/sleep apps consistently underperform developer tools and SaaS products on PH. The PH audience skews heavily toward tech founders, developers, and designers — not healthcare workers. Set expectations accordingly.

**Benchmark data from actual health/sleep app launches:**

| Product | Category | Upvotes | Ranking | Year |
|---------|----------|---------|---------|------|
| RISE by Rise Science | Sleep/Energy | 149 | #4 of Day | 2020 |
| Sleeptopia | Sleep/Apnea | 93 | Not featured | 2024 |
| Oura Ring 4 | Sleep/Wearable | 164 | Featured | 2025 |
| Welltory | Health/Wellness | ~200+ | Featured | Multiple launches |
| Remy AI | Sleep Coach | Top rated | Featured | 2024-2025 |

**For context — top PH launches overall:**
- Product of the Day typically requires 300-800+ upvotes (varies by day and competition)
- Product of the Week: ~1,000+ upvotes
- Product of the Month: ~2,000+ upvotes
- Product of the Year 2025 (Wordware): 7,700+ upvotes

### Realistic Targets for ShiftWell

| Outcome | Upvotes | Probability | What It Means |
|---------|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Solid launch | 100-200 | 60% | Good visibility, some press pickup, steady trickle of downloads |
| Featured / Top 5 | 200-500 | 25% | Product of the Day contender, significant traffic spike, press interest |
| Breakout | 500+ | 10% | Product of the Day, newsletter features, viral founder story potential |
| Underperform | <100 | 5% | Still valuable — you get a PH page, SEO juice, and learn for relaunch |

**Note:** Only ~10% of launches get featured at all since the 2024 algorithm change. Being featured matters more than raw upvote count — a featured product with 300 upvotes generates dramatically more traffic than a non-featured product with 1,000.

### Expected Traffic and Conversion

Based on health app PH launch data:

| Metric | Conservative | Optimistic | Breakout |
|--------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| PH page views | 2,000-5,000 | 5,000-15,000 | 15,000-50,000 |
| Website clicks | 500-1,500 | 1,500-5,000 | 5,000-15,000 |
| App Store visits | 200-800 | 800-3,000 | 3,000-10,000 |
| Downloads | 50-200 | 200-800 | 800-3,000 |
| Conversion to paid (yr 1) | 3-5% of downloads | 3-5% | 3-5% |

**Key insight:** PH is a credibility and awareness play, not a user acquisition firehose — especially for health apps. The real value is: press pickup, backlinks for SEO, social proof badges, and the founder story narrative that lives on long after launch day.

### How ShiftWell Can Outperform the Category Average

ShiftWell has structural advantages that most health apps on PH don't:

1. **Founder story is PH catnip.** "ER doc builds app at 3 AM between patients" is infinitely more compelling than "we raised a Series A and hired a growth team." PH rewards indie makers and authentic stories.

2. **Solves a specific, underserved problem.** "Sleep optimization for shift workers" is concrete and novel. Generic "sleep better" apps blend together. Specificity wins on PH.

3. **Science-backed without being boring.** The Two-Process Model and NIOSH protocols give you credibility that AI-wrapper sleep apps can't match. PH's curation team values "usefulness" and "novelty" — you have both.

4. **No wearable required.** This is a meaningful differentiator. Lower barrier to entry = more people can try it immediately after seeing it on PH.

5. **Transparent pricing.** PH users respect honest pricing ($29.99/yr with free tier) over dark-pattern trials.

### What a PH Launch Does NOT Do

Be clear-eyed about limitations:

- PH will not make you go viral overnight
- PH traffic is ephemeral — 80% of it happens in the first 48 hours
- PH users are not your target demographic (tech workers, not nurses)
- A #1 Product of the Day badge does not guarantee App Store success
- PH alone is not a growth strategy — it's a single event in a broader launch plan

**Use PH as an ignition point** for press outreach, social proof, and founder credibility. The real growth comes from the channels that follow: press coverage, App Store SEO, content marketing to shift worker communities, and word of mouth.

## 7. Promo Code Strategy

PH users love exclusive offers. Prepare a launch-day promo:

- **Offer:** "Product Hunt exclusive — 50% off first year of Premium ($14.99 instead of $29.99)"
- **Code:** PRODUCTHUNT (simple, memorable)
- **Expiration:** 7 days after launch
- **Where to mention:** In your first comment, on the PH promo code field, and in social posts
- **Why 50% off:** It's generous enough to drive conversions but still validates willingness to pay. At $14.99/yr, you're still well above $0 and building a paid user base from day one.
