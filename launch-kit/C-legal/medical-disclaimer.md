## Not Medical Advice

The recommendations and plans generated by ShiftWell are **for informational and general wellness purposes only**. They are **not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment**.

ShiftWell recommendations are based on published research in circadian science and sleep medicine, including:
- Two-Process Model of Sleep Regulation (Borbely 1982)
- American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) Guidelines (2015/2023)
- Circadian phase-shifting protocols (Eastman & Burgess 2009)
- Sleep timing and caffeine optimization (Drake et al. 2004, 2013)
- Napping strategies in shift work (Milner & Cote 2009; Ruggiero & Redeker 2014)
- NIOSH CDC Occupational Health Guidelines for shift workers
- Additional foundational research in circadian biology and sleep science

**However, these recommendations have not been independently validated in clinical trials specific to ShiftWell users.**

## Shift Work Sleep Disorder (SWSD)

If you experience persistent symptoms of Shift Work Sleep Disorder — such as severe insomnia during sleep periods, excessive daytime sleepiness, difficulty concentrating, or mood changes — **see a healthcare provider for diagnosis and treatment**.

ShiftWell does **not diagnose or treat SWSD**. It provides scheduling optimization that may support sleep quality, but it is not a substitute for medical care.

## No Guarantee of Results

**Individual results vary.** Sleep quality is affected by many factors beyond shift scheduling, including:
- Genetics and chronotype
- Overall health and medication use
- Environmental factors (light, noise, temperature)
- Stress and mental health
- Caffeine and alcohol intake
- Exercise and diet habits

ShiftWell cannot guarantee improved sleep or health outcomes. The app provides evidence-based recommendations; your results depend on many other factors outside the app's control.

## Safety Warning

**Your safety is non-negotiable.**

- **Never drive or operate machinery when drowsy**, regardless of what ShiftWell recommends. If you feel unsafe, do not drive.
- **Listen to your body.** If ShiftWell's recommendations conflict with how your body feels, prioritize your body's signals over the app.
- **Don't ignore warning signs.** If you experience severe fatigue, persistent insomnia, or other concerning symptoms, stop using the app and contact a healthcare provider immediately.
