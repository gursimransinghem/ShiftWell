# ShiftWell Data Disclosure

We believe transparency builds trust. Here's exactly what data ShiftWell collects, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

---

## What We Collect & What We Do With It

| **What We Collect** | **Why** | **How Long We Keep It** | **Who Sees It** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Account Info**<br>(email, name, password hash) | To create your account and let you sign in | Until you delete your account | Only ShiftWell |
| **Shift Schedule**<br>(work times, rotation patterns, days off) | To generate your personalized optimized sleep plan | Until you delete your account | Only ShiftWell |
| **Sleep Data**<br>(from WHOOP, HealthKit, or manual entry) | To track your sleep quality and adjust recommendations over time | Until you delete your account | Only ShiftWell |
| **Chronotype & Preferences**<br>(quiz results, household info, napping habits) | To personalize sleep recommendations based on your body's natural rhythm | Until you delete your account | Only ShiftWell |
| **Calendar Events**<br>(from Google Calendar sync) | To avoid scheduling sleep during your work, meetings, or commitments | Cached temporarily; refreshed each time you sync | Only ShiftWell |
| **Device Info**<br>(device type, OS version, timezone, app version) | To ensure the app works smoothly on your device | Until you delete your account | Only ShiftWell |
| **Usage Analytics**<br>(which features you use, crash reports, timestamps) | To fix bugs and understand how people use ShiftWell to make it better | Anonymized; kept for 12 months then deleted | Anonymized / aggregated only |
| **Subscription Info** | To manage your premium subscription and billing | Per Apple/Google app store policies | Apple/Google (payment processors) |

---

## Your Rights

**You have full control over your data.** At any time, you can:

- **Access** — View all your data in Settings > Your Data
- **Download** — Export your shift schedule, sleep data, and recommendations as a file
- **Correct** — Update any information that's wrong
- **Delete** — Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data

**California residents:** You have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Contact us to exercise these rights.

---

## What We DON'T Collect

We have no interest in tracking:
- **Location** — We don't know where you are
- **Contacts** — We don't access your phone's contact list
- **Photos or videos** — We don't use your camera or photo library
- **Microphone** — We don't record audio
- **Biometric data** — We don't collect fingerprints or facial recognition (WHOOP collects heart rate and HRV — we only receive your derived sleep score)
- **Browsing history** — We don't track what websites you visit
- **Advertising identifiers** — We don't use your IDFA or similar identifiers for ad tracking

---

## Data Security

**Your data is encrypted and protected.**

- **In transit:** All data traveling to/from ShiftWell servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- **At rest:** Your sleep data and shift schedule are encrypted in our Supabase database
- **Access controls:** Only authorized ShiftWell servers can access your data — not even our team can read it directly

We follow industry-standard security practices and conduct regular audits to keep your information safe.
