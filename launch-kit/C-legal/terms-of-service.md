## 2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

ShiftWell is a wellness application designed to help shift workers optimize their sleep schedules and circadian rhythm alignment. The App provides:

- **Schedule Analysis:** Import and analysis of your shift work schedule
- **Sleep Optimization Recommendations:** Science-backed sleep, nap, meal, and light exposure timing recommendations based on circadian rhythm science
- **Calendar Integration:** Export recommendations to your calendar and sync with third-party services (e.g., Apple HealthKit, Google Calendar, WHOOP)
