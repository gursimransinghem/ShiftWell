# ShiftWell — App Preview Video Script

> 30-second App Store Preview video. Plays on mute by default — visuals must tell the story alone.
> Captions are mandatory (Apple guidelines). Music enhances but doesn't carry.

---

## Technical Requirements

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Duration | 15-30 seconds (target: 28 seconds) |
| Resolution | 1290 × 2796 px (iPhone 15 Pro Max recording) |
| Format | H.264, .mov or .mp4 |
| Frame rate | 30 fps |
| Audio | AAC stereo, optional but recommended |
| Captions | Required (burned in or closed captions) |

---

## Mood & Direction

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Music | Ambient electronic, low tempo (70-80 BPM), calm but forward-moving. Think "focused at 3am" not "spa relaxation." Reference: similar to ODESZA instrumentals or Tycho's "Awake" |
| Color palette | Dark backgrounds (#0A0E1A), purple/blue accents, warm gold highlights |
| Pacing | Smooth transitions, no hard cuts. Each scene flows into the next via crossfade or slide |
| Text style | SF Pro Display, white on dark, left-aligned. Keywords appear as the screen demonstrates them |
| Tone | Confident, calm, empathetic. Not salesy. Not clinical. "We get it." |

---

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### SCENE 1 — THE PROBLEM (0:00-0:05)
**Visual:** Dark screen. Text fades in center-screen:
> "You work nights."
> _(1 beat)_
> "Your sleep app doesn't."

**Voiceover:** _(silence — text only)_
**Caption:** "You work nights. Your sleep app doesn't."
**Music:** Subtle bass pulse begins.

**Why:** Instant emotional hook. Every shift worker has tried a sleep app that assumed a 9-to-5 schedule. This names the frustration in 2 seconds.

---

### SCENE 2 — IMPORT (0:05-0:10)
**Visual:** Hand taps "Import Schedule." ICS file picker appears. File selected: "April_Shifts.ics." Preview shows: "12 shifts found — 4 day, 6 night, 2 swing." Confirm tap.

**Voiceover:** "Import your shift schedule in one tap."
**Caption:** "Import your shifts from any calendar"
**Music:** Melody enters — gentle synth pad.

**Why:** Shows effortless onboarding. The ICS count ("12 shifts found") proves the app understands complex schedules.

---

### SCENE 3 — PLAN GENERATED (0:10-0:17)
**Visual:** Calendar view populates with color-coded blocks. Camera slowly zooms into a single day showing:
- Purple sleep block: 08:00-15:30
- Blue nap: 18:00-18:45
- Amber shift: 19:00-07:00
- Caffeine cutoff badge pulses: "Last caffeine 02:00 AM"
- Meal window indicator: "Eat 20:00-01:00"

**Voiceover:** "ShiftWell builds your sleep plan — sleep windows, naps, caffeine cutoffs, meal timing — and writes it to your calendar."
**Caption:** "Your complete sleep plan, on your calendar"
**Music:** Beat develops, still ambient.

**Why:** This is the money shot. The calendar filling with blocks is visually satisfying and immediately communicates "this app does the work for you." Caffeine and meal timing show it's comprehensive, not just a bedtime reminder.

---

### SCENE 4 — SCIENCE (0:17-0:22)
**Visual:** Quick sequence (1.5s each):
1. Recovery Score ring filling to 82/100
2. Insight card: "Bedtime shifted because 3-night stretch starts Thursday"
3. Citation: "Based on 15+ peer-reviewed studies"

**Voiceover:** "Powered by the same circadian science used by NASA and the NFL."
**Caption:** "Circadian science. Not guesswork."
**Music:** Brief swell.

**Why:** Credibility in 5 seconds. Recovery Score shows ongoing value. The adaptive insight shows intelligence. NASA/NFL is aspirational social proof.

---

### SCENE 5 — CLOSER (0:22-0:28)
**Visual:** App icon centered on dark background. Text appears below:
> "ShiftWell"
> "Shift Work Sleep & Recovery"
> "Download free. Start in 60 seconds."

**Voiceover:** "ShiftWell. Sleep better. Work safer."
**Caption:** "ShiftWell — Sleep better. Work safer."
**Music:** Resolves to single sustained note, fades.

**Why:** Clean close. "Work safer" adds a dimension competitors don't touch — shift work sleep isn't just comfort, it's patient/public safety. "60 seconds" removes friction.

## Voiceover Specifications

| Spec | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Voice | Male or female, mid-30s, calm and assured. Not overly cheerful. Think "trusted colleague" not "infomercial host" |
| Pace | Measured, ~140 words per minute. Leave space for visuals to breathe |
| Accent | Neutral American English |
| Total words | ~45 words (fits comfortably in 28 seconds with pauses) |

**Full voiceover script:**
> "Import your shift schedule in one tap. ShiftWell builds your sleep plan — sleep windows, naps, caffeine cutoffs, meal timing — and writes it to your calendar. Powered by the same circadian science used by NASA and the NFL. ShiftWell. Sleep better. Work safer."
